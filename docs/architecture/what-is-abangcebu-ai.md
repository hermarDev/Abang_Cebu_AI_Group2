# What is AbangCebu AI?

**Source:** [Confluence: What is AbangCebu AI?](https://abangcebuai.atlassian.net/wiki/x/hIAB)
**Space:** SCRUM | **Page ID:** 98436

AbangCebu AI is a mobile-first, map-based boarding house and rental discovery platform designed for Metro Cebu.

It brings local rental listings into one organized platform where people can search for bedspaces, boarding houses, private rooms, studios, apartments, condominiums, and houses based on their actual needs.

The platform is intended primarily for students, BPO employees, young professionals, and people relocating within Metro Cebu. It also provides landlords with a structured way to publish and maintain their rental listings.


## Why AbangCebu AI Exists

Finding an affordable place to stay in Metro Cebu can be difficult because rental information is scattered across Facebook groups, Marketplace posts, personal messages, handwritten signs, and word-of-mouth recommendations.

These methods often create several problems:

- Listings do not provide an accurate map location.
- Available rooms may already be occupied.
- Prices may not clearly indicate whether they are per person, per bed, or for the entire room.
- Important expenses, such as electricity, water, advance payment, and security deposits, may be missing.
- Rental rules regarding cooking, laundry, visitors, curfew, gender, and occupancy may not be stated.
- Renters must repeatedly ask landlords whether a property is still available.
- It is difficult to compare several rentals consistently.
- Fake or misleading listings can be difficult to identify.
- Small landlords have no dedicated place to organize their listings and availability.

AbangCebu AI addresses these problems by creating a centralized and structured rental-discovery experience specifically designed around the needs of Cebu renters.


## How It Works

A finder opens AbangCebu and is immediately presented with a map of Metro Cebu. Available rentals are displayed using price markers and listing cards.

The finder can search manually by choosing:

- Preferred location or landmark
- Search distance
- Minimum and maximum budget
- Rental or property type
- Number of occupants
- Move-in date
- Bathroom preference
- Tenant or gender policy
- Cooking and laundry rules
- Curfew and visitor policies
- Utilities and additional charges
- Parking options
- Desired amenities

The finder can also ask the AbangCebu AI rental assistant using natural language.

For example:

> “I need a private room near Cebu IT Park for two people. My budget is ₱5,000, and cooking should be allowed.”

The assistant interprets the request, identifies the selected location, and searches the platform’s current approved rental listings. It then presents suitable rental recommendations as cards connected to the same locations shown on the map.

The finder can continue the conversation with requests such as:

- “Only show rentals with Wi-Fi.”
- “I need my own bathroom.”
- “Increase my budget to ₱6,000.”
- “Show places without a curfew.”
- “Find something closer to USC Talamban.”
- “We need a room that allows two occupants.”

When a location is unclear, the assistant asks the finder to clarify instead of guessing. For example, if the finder says “near UC,” the assistant may ask whether they mean UC Main, UC Banilad, or UC Lapu-Lapu and Mandaue.


## The Role of AI

The AI assistant acts as a conversational guide between the finder and the rental database.

It helps users express their requirements naturally, even when their request includes a mixture of English and Cebuano, such as:

> “Nangita kog room near IT Park, good for two, budget ₱5,000, pwede maka-luto, and no curfew.”

The assistant converts the request into structured rental-search criteria. It does not create, assume, or invent properties.

Every rental recommendation must come from an actual approved listing stored in AbangCebu. The AI cannot change prices, publish properties, approve listings, update availability, or manage user accounts.

If no listing matches the request, the assistant informs the finder and suggests possible adjustments. The finder remains in control of whether to expand the location, increase the budget, or remove a preference.


## Main Users


### Finders

Finders are people looking for accommodation in Metro Cebu, including students, employees, families, and individuals relocating for school or work.

They can:

- Explore rentals on the map
- Search near schools, workplaces, and landmarks
- Apply detailed rental filters
- Ask the AI assistant for recommendations
- Compare rental prices, locations, terms, and amenities
- View property photos and information
- Check landlord-reported availability
- Contact the landlord through the published contact option

People can explore available rentals without being required to create an account immediately.


### Landlords and Property Listers

Landlords use AbangCebu to present and manage their rental properties in a more organized way.

They can:

- Publish rooms, bedspaces, apartments, and other rental properties
- Place the property’s location on the map
- Upload property photos
- Specify the monthly rent and rental basis
- Indicate room capacity and accepted occupants
- Declare amenities, house rules, utility arrangements, and additional charges
- Publish their preferred contact method
- Update whether a unit is available, occupied, or temporarily paused

This helps reduce repetitive inquiries because renters can view important rental information before contacting the landlord.


### Administrators

Administrators help maintain the quality and reliability of the platform.

They can:

- Review submitted rental listings
- Approve, reject, or suspend listings
- Manage finder and landlord accounts
- Respond to reported listings or users
- Maintain Metro Cebu landmarks and their common names
- Review important moderation activities
- Remove inappropriate or misleading public information

Listing approval means that a listing has passed the platform’s moderation process. It does not replace an in-person property visit or guarantee the identity, ownership, or condition of a property.


## What Makes AbangCebu Different?

AbangCebu is not simply a general online marketplace with a chatbot added to it. It is designed specifically for local rental discovery.

Its main differences include:

- **Map-first discovery:** Rentals are presented according to their actual location, not only as a list of posts.
- **Cebu-focused searches:** The platform recognizes local schools, workplaces, barangays, business districts, and commonly used landmark names.
- **Real rental requirements:** Searches consider practical concerns such as occupancy, bathroom privacy, cooking, laundry, curfew, utilities, deposits, tenant policies, and move-in dates.
- **Database-grounded AI:** AI recommendations come only from current approved system listings.
- **Connected map and recommendations:** A rental recommended by the assistant corresponds to the same listing card and map location.
- **Structured rental information:** Listings follow a consistent format, making them easier to understand and compare.
- **Availability management:** Landlords can update occupancy without deleting and reposting their property.
- **Manual search independence:** The map and filters remain useful even when the AI assistant is unavailable.
- **Direct communication:** AbangCebu helps finders discover suitable rentals and connect with landlords, but it does not replace direct property viewing and rental agreements.


## Initial Area of Coverage

AbangCebu focuses on the Metro Cebu urban area, including:

- Cebu City
- Mandaue City
- Lapu-Lapu City
- Talisay City

Searches can be centered around places such as universities, colleges, business districts, hospitals, transportation areas, and other recognized landmarks.

Examples include:

- Cebu IT Park
- Cebu Business Park
- University of Cebu campuses
- University of San Carlos campuses
- Cebu Institute of Technology–University
- Cebu Normal University
- ACLC campuses
- Nearby barangays and residential communities


## Platform Boundaries

AbangCebu is a rental-discovery and listing-management platform. It does not:

- Process rent or reservation payments
- Hold deposits or provide escrow services
- Automatically create lease agreements
- Guarantee a property’s physical condition
- Guarantee landlord identity or property ownership
- Calculate live traffic or exact travel time
- Make administrative decisions through AI
- Allow AI to modify rental or user records

Finders should still conduct an ocular visit, confirm the landlord’s identity, inspect the property, review rental terms, and avoid sending money before completing the necessary checks.


## Project Goal

The goal of AbangCebu AI is to make rental discovery in Metro Cebu more organized, transparent, location-aware, and convenient.

It connects three important activities in one platform:

> Finders search for the right place, landlords maintain accurate rental information, and administrators help keep the marketplace organized and trustworthy.


### Short Description

> AbangCebu AI is a Metro Cebu-focused rental finder that combines an interactive map, structured rental listings, detailed filters, and a database-grounded AI assistant to help people find suitable accommodation and connect directly with local landlords.
