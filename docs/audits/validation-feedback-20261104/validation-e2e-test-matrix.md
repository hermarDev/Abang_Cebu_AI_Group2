## Cross-cutting risk scenarios

- Validation: equivalence classes, empty/whitespace, malformed email, `min−1/min/max/max+1`, long/multibyte and first value over byte/count limit; compare client and server outputs without changing policy.
- Mutations: success plus authoritative persistence read and reload; error and unchanged data, double-submit, cancelled confirmation, timeout before/after commit, stable operation identity and conflict handling. Never retry a non-idempotent mutation based solely on a missing response.
- Authorization: guest, expected role, wrong role, suspended and other user's object, stale account identifier; exercise direct API/RPC and effective RLS with isolated synthetic identities, not forged cookies or production access.
- Browser: phone (including short landscape), tablet and desktop, light/dark, keyboard-only, zoom/reflow, reduced motion, visible focus, assistive descriptions; notifications must not obscure nav, map controls or attribution. Manual screen-reader/device coverage is separate from automated DOM checks.
